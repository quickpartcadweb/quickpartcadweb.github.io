QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is the website really $99?

$99 USD is the introductory price for up to five customized static HTML, CSS and JavaScript pages within an agreed scope. Domain, hosting, publishing/setup and extra features cost separately.

Do I need to buy a domain?

A custom domain is optional, but recommended for your business. Domain purchase and renewal are separate from the design price.

Does this include online payments or bookings?

No. The base package is a static informational website. E-commerce, dynamic booking or integrations require a separate quote.

Can I use my own photos and logo?

Yes. We'll work with materials you provide and approve, provided you have the right to use them.

Is the contact form in the demo working?

No. The fictional demonstration is for design review. For a real website we can link your email and phone; a hosted functional form or other service is a separately scoped feature.

Will my website appear in Google or Bing?

Our optional $50 Search-Ready Setup prepares the technical files, helps connect search tools and submits live pages for discovery. Search engines decide whether and when to index or rank any page; we cannot guarantee an appearance in results.

How do I get started?

Email quickpartcad@gmail.com with your business name, services, existing online listings and any photos or brand references. We'll discuss a suitable scope.
